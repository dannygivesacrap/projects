Questions?

We've got answers

The stuff everyone asks us (and some things nobody asked but we wanted to say anyway)

Why is it called SPLOOGE?
Have you ever squeezed a concentrated detergent bottle? That's the sound it makes. Also, it's hilarious and you'll never forget it. You're welcome.
Is one splooge really enough?
Yes. We promise. Our formula is 6x more concentrated than regular detergent. One splooge (about 1 tablespoon) handles a full load. For extra dirty loads or larger machines, you can do 1.5 splooges. Live dangerously.
Is it safe for HE machines?
Absolutely. SPLOOGE is low-sudsing by design, which makes it perfect for high-efficiency machines. It also works great in standard machines. We don't discriminate against appliances.
What about sensitive skin?
Our Clean Slate (unscented) formula is specifically designed for sensitive skin. No dyes, no fragrances, no nonsense. The scented versions use skin-safe fragrance oils, but if you've got allergies, go Clean Slate.
Can I recycle the bottle?
Yes! Aluminum is infinitely recyclable and is one of the most recycled materials on the planet. Just rinse it out and toss it in your recycling bin. Or keep it and we'll send you refill pouches (coming soon).
Do you ship internationally?
We're currently shipping to the US, Canada, and the UK. We're working on expanding to more countries, but international shipping is tricky when you're trying to minimize carbon footprint. Stay tuned.

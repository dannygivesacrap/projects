Why is it called SPLOOGE?
Squeeze the bottle. That's the sound. Also, you'll never forget it. Marketing 101: be memorable. You're thinking about laundry detergent right now. We win.
Is one splooge actually enough?
Yes. We promise. 6X concentrated means one splooge (~1 tablespoon) handles a full load. For extra filthy loads, go 1.5 splooges. But seriously, resist the urge to go ham. More isn't better here.
Works with HE machines?
Absolutely. Low-sudsing by design. Works with HE, standard, front-load, top-load, whatever. We don't discriminate against appliances.
Sensitive skin?
Clean Slate (unscented) is your friend. No dyes, no fragrances, no nonsense. The scented versions use skin-safe fragrance oils, but if you've got allergies, play it safe.
Can I recycle the bottle?
Yes. Aluminum is infinitely recyclable. Rinse it, toss it in the bin. Or keep it for our refill pouches (coming soon). Or turn it into modern art. Your call.
International shipping?
US, Canada, UK for now. We're working on more, but shipping heavy stuff internationally kind of defeats the whole "reduce carbon footprint" thing. Stay tuned.

Questions? Bet.

Why is it called Splooge?
Squeeze the bottle. That's the sound. Also you'll literally never forget it. You're thinking about laundry detergent right now. We win.
Is one splooge really enough?
Yes bestie. 6X concentrated means one splooge (~1 tablespoon) handles a full load. For extra gross loads, do 1.5 splooges. But seriously, more is not more here.
Works with my HE machine?
Absolutely. Low-sudsing queen energy. Works with HE, standard, front-load, top-load, whatever you've got. We don't discriminate.
Sensitive skin?
Clean Slate (unscented) is your bestie. No dyes, no fragrances, no drama. The scented ones use skin-safe fragrance oils, but if you're sensitive, go Clean Slate.
Can I recycle the bottle?
Duh! Aluminum is infinitely recyclable. Rinse it, toss it. Or keep it for our refill pouches (coming soon). Or make it into art. Your call.
Do you ship internationally?
US, Canada, UK for now. We're working on more, but shipping heavy stuff internationally kinda defeats the whole carbon footprint thing. Stay tuned.

Questions?

We've got answers

Why is it called Splooge?
Have you ever squeezed a concentrated detergent bottle? That's the sound it makes. Also, you'll never forget it. You're thinking about laundry detergent right now. We win.
Is one splooge really enough?
Yes. We promise. Our formula is 6x more concentrated than regular detergent. One splooge (about 1 tablespoon) handles a full load. For extra dirty loads, you can do 1.5 splooges. Live dangerously.
Is it safe for HE machines?
Absolutely. Splooge is low-sudsing by design, which makes it perfect for high-efficiency machines. It also works great in standard machines. We don't discriminate against appliances.
What about sensitive skin?
Our Clean Slate (unscented) formula is designed for sensitive skin. No dyes, no fragrances, no nonsense. The scented versions use skin-safe fragrance oils, but if you've got allergies, go Clean Slate.
Can I recycle the bottle?
Yes! Aluminum is infinitely recyclable. Just rinse it out and toss it in your recycling bin. Or keep it for our refill pouches (coming soon). Or turn it into art. Your call.
Do you ship internationally?
We're currently shipping to the US, Canada, and the UK. We're working on expanding, but international shipping kind of defeats the "reduce carbon footprint" thing. Stay tuned.

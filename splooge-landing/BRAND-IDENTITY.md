# SPLOOGE Brand Identity Guide

## The Name
Yes, we went there. SPLOOGE is bold, memorable, and impossible to ignore—just like what happens when you squeeze our hyper-concentrated formula. One small splooge is all you need.

---

## Brand Positioning

**Tagline:** *A little goes a long way.*

**Secondary taglines:**
- "Enzyme-powered clean. Earth-approved packaging."
- "Less goop. More clean."
- "The last detergent you'll ever love."

---

## Brand Voice & Tone

### Personality Traits
- **Irreverent but not immature** — We make jokes, but we're serious about clean clothes and a clean planet
- **Friendly and direct** — Talk like a clever friend, not a corporation
- **Confidently weird** — Own the name. Lean into the double-takes
- **Sustainably smug** — We're doing good for the planet and we're not shy about it

### Writing Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use puns liberally (splooge, squirt, squeeze, drip—have fun)
- Be conversational and warm
- Explain science simply, without dumbing it down
- Celebrate the absurdity of caring deeply about laundry
- Include the occasional wink at the name

**Don't:**
- Be crude or genuinely offensive
- Use corporate jargon
- Greenwash—be specific about sustainability claims
- Take yourself too seriously

### Example Copy

**Hero headline:**
> "One splooge. That's it. Your clothes will thank you."

**Product description:**
> "SPLOOGE is a hyper-concentrated, enzyme-powered laundry formula that actually works. We're talking 100+ loads from one sleek aluminum bottle. No plastic jugs. No measuring cup disasters. No weird chemical smell. Just clean clothes that smell like you walked through a fancy garden (but, like, a cool one)."

**Sustainability message:**
> "Our bottles are aluminum because we're not monsters. Refill it, recycle it, turn it into a tiny robot—we don't judge. What we do judge: single-use plastic. Hard pass."

**FAQ tone:**
> **Q: Why is it called SPLOOGE?**
> A: Have you ever squeezed a concentrated detergent bottle? That's the sound it makes. Also, it's hilarious and you'll never forget it. You're welcome.

## Visual Identity

### Logo
The SPLOOGE wordmark is bold, friendly, and slightly playful. Uses a custom sans-serif with rounded terminals and a subtle "splash" element on the O that suggests a droplet.

### Color Palette

#### Primary Colors
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Splooge Blue** | `#2D5BFF` | Primary brand color, CTAs, accents |
| **Cream** | `#FFF9F0` | Backgrounds, breathing room |
| **Deep Navy** | `#0A1628` | Text, contrast elements |

#### Secondary Colors
| Color | Hex | Usage |
|-------|-----|-------|
| **Enzyme Green** | `#7FE5A0` | Highlights, success states, eco messaging |
| **Sunset Coral** | `#FF6B5B` | Accent, energy, urgency |
| **Lavender Haze** | `#C4B5FF` | Scent representation, soft accents |

### Typography

**Headlines:** Obviously Sans (or Inter as fallback) — Bold, confident, geometric
**Body:** Inter — Clean, highly readable, friendly
**Accents:** A hand-drawn or playful script for callouts and emphasis

### Photography Style
- Bright, airy, lots of natural light
- Real homes, real laundry situations (not sterile studios)
- The aluminum bottle as hero object—show it in kitchens, laundry rooms, on shelves
- Close-ups of the satisfying squeeze/splooge moment
- Lifestyle shots with diverse, real people who look like they actually do laundry

### Packaging
- **Material:** Aluminum bottle (matte finish, premium feel)
- **Label:** Minimal, bold wordmark, single accent color on cream base
- **Closure:** Precision squeeze cap for controlled dispensing
- **Sizes:** 16oz (100+ loads), 32oz (200+ loads)

## Brand Values

### 1. Radical Concentration
Why ship water? Our formula is 6x more concentrated than traditional detergents. Less packaging, less shipping weight, less waste. More clean.

### 2. Enzyme Intelligence
Our plant-derived enzymes break down stains at the molecular level. No harsh chemicals, no optical brighteners that just coat your clothes in plastic film. Actual clean.

### 3. Packaging That Doesn't Suck
Aluminum is infinitely recyclable and doesn't leach microplastics into your laundry. Plus it looks damn good on your shelf.

### 4. Delicious Scents
We worked with perfumers (actual perfumers) to create scents you'll want to smell on purpose. No "ocean breeze" that smells like a gas station bathroom.
